Come hungry. Louisville is the kind of food city where one bite can take you around the world, and there's no bad season for the journey. Sure, the city invented the gloriously gooey Hot Brown, and bourbon has a starring role, but Louisville's culinary personality goes far beyond its Kentucky classics.
