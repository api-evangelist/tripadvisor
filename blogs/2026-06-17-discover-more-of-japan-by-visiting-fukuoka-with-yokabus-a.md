Nestled in Kyushu, Japan's third-largest island, Fukuoka Prefecture is a crossroads between cultures. In the dynamic cities of Fukuoka and Kitakyushu, contemporary life meets the tranquillity of ancient traditions. Beyond these cities, Fukuoka’s lesser-known spots—from rural villages to lush forests, scenic mountains, and ancient shrines—await discovery.
