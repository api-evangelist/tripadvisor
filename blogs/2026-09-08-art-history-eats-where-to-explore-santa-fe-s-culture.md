Santa Fe is often touted as the cultural capital of the Southwestern United States. And whether you’re dodging the crowds with a mid-week trip or diving into the bustling city for a more extended stay, it doesn’t take long to see how the city earned such a title. Explore adobe-bricked buildings dating back hundreds of years, savor innovative dishes inspired by millennia-old culinary traditions, and absorb art from icons and rising stars alike—all in a city that somehow defies time itself.
