Round up the whole crew, because Columbia doesn't ask any generation to sit this one out. Grandparents, parents, and kids can each find something they love here, and you won't spend half your trip negotiating whose turn it is to pick the activity. Columbia is easy and affordable to visit, with enough variety packed into one walkable city that your family can build a single shared itinerary instead of splitting into separate trips.
