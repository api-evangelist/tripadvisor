One of the best things about basing a trip in Atlanta is how quickly the city gives way to something completely different. Within an hour or two in almost any direction, you're in the mountains, wandering a historic small-town square, or pulling over on a back road because the view demands it. This three-day itinerary shows you how to do it all without ever needing to repack your bag.
