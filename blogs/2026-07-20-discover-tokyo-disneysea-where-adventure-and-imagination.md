Discover a world of adventure and imagination at Tokyo DisneySea, the only Disney Park in the world that is inspired by the sea. The impressive theme park paves the way for never-before-seen experiences, attractions, and food. Explore eight themed ports, where you’ll glide across the Mediterranean Harbor’s Palazzo Canals and step into the enchanting world of the park’s latest themed port, Fantasy Springs.
