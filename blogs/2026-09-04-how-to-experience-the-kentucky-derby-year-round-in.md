Every spring, Louisville comes alive with nearly two weeks of Kentucky Derby Festival events leading up to the Kentucky Oaks and Kentucky Derby, from parades and fireworks to food, fashion, and celebrations across the city. The Kentucky Derby, held at Churchill Downs since 1875, is America's longest continuously held sporting event and the first jewel of the Triple Crown. More than "the most exciting two minutes in sports," it's a beloved tradition woven into the fabric of Louisville's culture.
