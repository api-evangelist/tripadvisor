Tokyo Disneyland is a theme park that brings stories to life with a touch of magic. Around every corner of the carefully designed theme park, it feels like you’ve jumped straight into the storybook with music drifting through the streets and an added warmth of Japanese hospitality. Familiar Disney tales are reimagined with an added sparkle.
