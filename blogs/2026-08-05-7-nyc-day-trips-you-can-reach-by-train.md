It’s true that New York City has nearly everything you need—there’s no shortage of things to do, eat, and see. But sometimes, it’s a good idea to leave the bustling streets, even if just for a bit. And the beauty of the city is that you don't need a car or extra PTO to break the spell—all you really need is a train ticket and a few hours (at most).
