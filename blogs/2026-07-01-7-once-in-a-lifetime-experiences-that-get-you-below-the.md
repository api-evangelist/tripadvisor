There's something special about being underwater—time slows down and you feel weightless, so thoroughly removed from the world above. Here, we’ve rounded up seven once-in-a-lifetime experiences that take that feeling and dial it all the way up. Think: sleeping on the ocean floor, drifting between two tectonic plates, coming (safely) face-to-face with a great white shark.
